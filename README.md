## About NSD

**Neuro Software Distribution** is an independent software and systems collective
dedicated to answering questions nobody responsible was asking.

We build software because existing software is interesting.

Then we inspect the software underneath it because apparently leaving abstractions
alone is not an option.

Projects range from terminal applications and numerical libraries to networking,
audio systems, simulations, hardware experiments, and other incidents that began
with the sentence:

> *"Wait, how hard could that actually be?"*

Most of them eventually find out.

## What We Make

### Cyberspace

A small web project that made the tactical error of surviving long enough to develop
authentication, databases, Markdown, templates, music streaming and opinions.

### ShittyJukeBox Ω²

A terminal music player created after somebody decided that simply playing an MP3
was not sufficiently complicated.

Audio decoding, playlists, synchronized lyrics, FFT visualization, MPRIS integration
and other completely proportionate responses followed.

### Big Nums

Arbitrary-precision arithmetic for situations where ordinary integers have failed
to demonstrate sufficient ambition.

Includes integers, floating-point arithmetic, FFT-backed multiplication and an
unhealthy distrust of incorrect answers.

### Experimental Systems

Physics, graphics, networking, retro games, interpreters, allocators, hardware
experiments and assorted attempts to discover where the abstraction layer actually ends.

Some become products.

Some become documentation.

Some become warnings.

## Corporate Philosophy

NSD development traditionally follows a highly optimized seven-stage process:

1. **Find something cool.**
2. **Use it.**
3. **Become suspicious of it.**
4. **Find out how it works.**
5. **Implement part of it ourselves.**
6. **Accidentally triple the project scope.**
7. **Give it an increasingly stupid name.**

This methodology has demonstrated a 100% success rate at producing more work.

## Internal Structure

**Neuro™** operates primarily above the abstraction boundary.

Software, interfaces, algorithms, creative systems, audio, networking and the
general transformation of ideas into working programs fall under R&D.

**Crash** operates primarily near or below that boundary.

Hardware, instruction sets, assembly, embedded systems, architecture and future
bare-metal experiments fall under Hardware & Architecture.

Occasional jurisdiction disputes are resolved by whichever one already opened
the datasheet.

## Quality Assurance

Every NSD product undergoes our rigorous internal certification program.

**NSD CERTIFIED™** means that at least one of the following has occurred:

- it compiled
- it survived long enough to become useful
- somebody wrote documentation
- somebody else asked to use it
- deleting it would now be emotionally difficult

Certification standards are subject to change without notice.
